Five sections, one product

Every Creator Radar audit follows the same five sections. The numbers inside come from your category and your competitors, read on the day we run it.

  1. 01

    How much of the coverage is bought

    We sample the videos in your category and count the ones carrying a disclosed sponsorship. You see how active the paid side of your category is.

  2. 02

    Who covers your category today

    Every channel we read, plotted by subscribers against the views a typical upload gets. The channels that out-reach their own subscriber count stand out.

    The creators we would approach for your product, in order, with their recent uploads, typical views and whether a competitor has sponsored them.

03 · THE FINE PRINT

What the audit leaves out

It is a read of what is already public on YouTube. Every count is a floor, because a search only returns so many videos.

  • No forecasts. The audit shows what creators already did. It does not predict installs or revenue.
  • No rate cards. Creator prices are not public, so none are printed.
  • No audience geography. YouTube does not publish it per channel. We ask creators for it when a booking gets serious.
  • YouTube only, for now. TikTok and Twitch are not part of this audit.
  • Games and apps only. We do not run audits for agencies or for gambling products.
