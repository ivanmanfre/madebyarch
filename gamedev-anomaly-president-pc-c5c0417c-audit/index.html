How this page was built
  • Public YouTube API metadata, read 15 October 2026. Counts are a snapshot and may change.
  • Video search ran through a third-party YouTube search service with the same queries and date windows; view, channel and paid-promotion figures come from the YouTube Data API.
  • Search results are bounded by the recorded queries and dates; no all-time volume estimate is available.
  • Paid-promotion status reflects the API flag declared by the uploader where available. The watch-page overlay was not independently checked. Missing flags are unknown; an undeclared placement may still be paid.
  • Off-topic matches are excluded from target-game sample counts. Official lifetime facts and adjacent-game evidence remain separate.
  • 1 video from 1 channel was left out of every count, chart and table on this page. A review of each sampled channel and its titles placed that channel as re-posted footage, spam, or giveaway and scam bait with no contribution of its own.
  • 1 video from 1 channel run by the game's developer, publisher or a platform holder was left out of every coverage count, share, chart and table on this page, which count third-party channels only. The official channel figures are shown separately.
  • 5 sampled channels with 6 videos speak to audiences outside the markets this report covers, as placed by the same channel review. Those videos stay in every count and in the full table; the channels are not drawn on the chart, in the hero or mosaic, or as creator cards.
  • Search queries: anomaly president, anomaly president gameplay, anomaly president early access, anomaly president review, anomaly president guide, anomaly president tips, anomaly president update, anomaly president roguelike, anomaly president steam, anomaly president phew phew games.
  • Content labels were assigned from title and channel evidence. A second independent pass re-checked a sample of the records. Adjacent creators have separate search and recent-upload evidence; they do not enter target-game totals.

This scan does not estimate audience demographics, watch time, creator fees or campaign results. Public metadata does not independently verify footage ownership. Absence from a recorded sample does not establish absence of all past coverage.
