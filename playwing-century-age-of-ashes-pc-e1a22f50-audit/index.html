How this page was built
  • Public YouTube API metadata: the target-game sample, its channel figures and receipts read 15 October 2026; creator shortlist channel figures, receipts and recent uploads read 28 October 2026. Counts are a snapshot and may change.
  • Video search ran through a third-party YouTube search service with the same queries and date windows; view, channel and paid-promotion figures come from the YouTube Data API.
  • Search results are bounded by the recorded queries and dates; no all-time volume estimate is available.
  • Paid-promotion status reflects the API flag declared by the uploader where available. The watch-page overlay was not independently checked. Missing flags are unknown; an undeclared placement may still be paid.
  • Off-topic matches are excluded from target-game sample counts. Adjacent-game evidence remains separate.
  • 1 sampled channel with 2 videos speaks to audiences outside the markets this report covers, as placed by the same channel review. Those videos stay in every count and in the full table; the channels are not drawn on the chart, in the hero or mosaic, or as creator cards.
  • Search queries: century age of ashes, century age of ashes gameplay, century age of ashes guide, century age of ashes tips, century age of ashes review, century age of ashes update, century age of ashes season, century age of ashes best class, century age of ashes pvp, century age of ashes beginner.
  • Content labels were assigned from title and channel evidence. A second independent pass re-checked a sample of the records. Adjacent creators have separate search and recent-upload evidence; they do not enter target-game totals.

This scan does not estimate audience demographics, watch time, creator fees or campaign results. Public metadata does not independently verify footage ownership. Absence from a recorded sample does not establish absence of all past coverage.
