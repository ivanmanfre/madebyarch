How this page was built
  • Everything on YouTube here came from the YouTube Data API v3 (search.list, videos.list, channels.list), read on 17 November 2026. No logged-in session, no third-party estimate.
  • The sample is four category searches, page one of each. A search returns at most 50 videos, so every count here is a floor, never a market size: 95 distinct videos across 40 channels, published in the 180 days to 17 November 2026. The four category searches returned 190 videos inside the window, 95 of them long-form. Every long-form video inside the window cleared the sample floors. YouTube’s own total-results figure is never printed.
  • A placement is a window video, long-form and five minutes or longer, where a researched brand won a disclosure phrase within 250 characters of its own name. Every competitor count on this page carries that five-minute floor, and the 03 map counts your own deals without it, so a shorter sponsored video of yours is named rather than dropped. The platform paid label on its own attributes to nobody, and a phrase that names its party inline is spent on that party. A firm placement carries the paid label or a sponsor sentence; a creator-code-only disclosure is counted separately as a code deal, and only with the brand’s own code or offer on the brand’s own line: a line the creator calls affiliate or non-affiliate, a video whose creator says no brand paid, a referral or affiliate-programme link, a link to somebody else and a livestream are never a deal. A brand named with no attributable disclosure is a mention, never a placement: 723 mentions were discarded on that rule.
  • Before any of that, each channel’s standing description text is blanked out: any line or 80-character run appearing in at least half of its stored descriptions, once it has five of them. An evergreen affiliate link counts as standing text, so a brand that survives only inside it is recorded and not counted as a deal: 95 window videos landed there. Two exceptions, as the build review read them: on a paid-labelled video a repeated line carrying the brand’s own offer counts when nothing in the video ties the label to anyone else, and on any video a line in which the creator states that the brand sponsors or partners with them counts beside the brand’s own link or code.
  • We read each channel’s 50 most recent uploads, so every per-channel count is a floor as well. A channel’s median is the views of the last 10 long-form uploads it published inside the window, where long-form means at least 300 seconds and not a livestream, and on an even count the median is the mean of the two middle values. Fewer than 5 leaves no median and no tier: 101 read channels sit there, unmeasured rather than under the floor. Shorts under 60 seconds are counted once, separately, and sit in no median: 38 in the window sample.
