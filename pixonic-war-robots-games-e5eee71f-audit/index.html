How this page was built
  • Public YouTube API metadata: the target-game sample, its channel figures and receipts read 15 October 2026; creator shortlist channel figures, receipts and recent uploads read 26 October 2026. Counts are a snapshot and may change.
  • Video search ran through a third-party YouTube search service with the same queries and date windows; view, channel and paid-promotion figures come from the YouTube Data API.
  • Search results are bounded by the recorded queries and dates; no all-time volume estimate is available.
  • Paid-promotion status reflects the API flag declared by the uploader where available. The watch-page overlay was not independently checked. Missing flags are unknown; an undeclared placement may still be paid.
  • Off-topic matches are excluded from target-game sample counts. Adjacent-game evidence remains separate.
  • 4 videos from 4 channels were left out of every count, chart and table on this page. A review of each sampled channel and its titles placed those channels as distributing modified game files or cheat tools or as re-posted footage, spam, or giveaway and scam bait with no contribution of its own.
  • 3 videos from 1 channel run by the game's developer, publisher or a platform holder were left out of every coverage count, share, chart and table on this page, which count third-party channels only.
  • 31 sampled channels with 80 videos speak to audiences outside the markets this report covers, as placed by the same channel review. Those videos stay in every count and in the full table; the channels are not drawn on the chart, in the hero or mosaic, or as creator cards.
  • Search queries: war robots, war robots gameplay, war robots guide, war robots tips, war robots update, war robots best build, war robots new robot, war robots tier list, war robots beginner, walking war robots.
  • Content labels were assigned from title and channel evidence. A second independent pass re-checked a sample of the records. Adjacent creators have separate search and recent-upload evidence; they do not enter target-game totals.
