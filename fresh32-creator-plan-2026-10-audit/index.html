Proposed first test

Try the product.
Then test the response.

Begin with Shaadi and Sarina, subject to the checks above. Give each the same toothbrush offer and a distinct content angle.

01

Let them try the toothbrush.

Send a sample and ask for honest feedback before agreeing a paid post. Confirm the audience can buy and receive the product. The trial should determine whether a recommendation makes sense.

02

Brief a demonstration for each.

Propose a practical product review for Shaadi and a daily-routine demonstration for Sarina. Keep the offer and landing page consistent, with room for each creator’s own opinion. Agree disclosure, usage rights and the publishing plan before booking.

03

Measure orders and acquisition cost.

Use a separate tracked link for each creator and agree an attribution window before launch. Record clicks, attributed first orders and revenue; compare creator fees, samples and shipping with the orders attributed to each post.

The decision after the test

Look at attributed orders, acquisition cost and what customers bought. Use that evidence to decide whether to repeat an angle, adjust the offer or try another creator. The public view counts on this page are a shortlist signal; they don’t forecast sales.

  • Scope. This is a research shortlist for a toothbrush trial alongside the creator programme FRESH 32 is building. The English-language research uses the United Kingdom, Slovenia and Croatia as the supported shipping scope. Creator content alone does not establish audience geography.
  • Sources and dates. Public YouTube video and channel records, Instagram partnership labels and public Instagram and TikTok posts, read on 18 October 2026. The YouTube search window runs from 21 April to 18 October 2026. Linked receipts identify the posts behind the figures.
  • YouTube limits. The category sample contains 6 long-form videos. Search and channel counts reflect the material read. Known countries outside the configured region were excluded, and the remaining sample was checked for English-language evidence. Each search returns at most 50 videos; channel histories cover up to 50 uploads. A long-form video is at least 300 seconds and is not a livestream.
  • Creator checks. Each proposed creator has ten recent owner posts behind the median. At least half are on category, the median exceeds 3,000 views and the latest post is within 90 days. Independent reads checked content and audience fit. Existing FRESH 32 relationships remain to confirm; no FRESH 32 disclosure was found in the posts checked.
