Who we’d test

Each pick has published work that shows how they could introduce local primary care. The city and format depend on who is watching and where Corner Health has appointments.

Kidizona

Lindsay Valerio · Chandler and Gilbert

Creator site

Valerio publishes guides for East Valley parents. Her September guide to the Arizona Oral Cancer Walk shows how she explains a local health event to families. Her fall guide includes Gilbert.

Test an Instagram Reel and Story. Show Chandler adults where to find a primary care practice and how to check insurance and availability. Put the Chandler provider page in the Story link sticker.

Arianny Valles

Pendiente Mi Gente · Phoenix

Show and sponsorship formats

Valles hosts a Spanish-language community show on La Onda and offers sponsored segments. Her interview on a local mentorship program shows the format. Corner Health lists Phoenix primary care providers who see patients in Spanish.

Test a sponsored Spanish-language segment. Have Valles walk through how someone can find a provider who sees patients in Spanish. Check the booking path and confirm the participating practices have openings before recording.

Living in Arizona Now

Relocation videos · Tempe and the East Valley

YouTube channel

The channel’s October newcomer guide already covers finding healthcare after a move. It also published a Tempe tour in November.

Test a short segment in a relocation video. Put finding a primary care provider on a move-in checklist and link to the Tempe provider page. Ask how much of the audience has already moved or plans to move soon before booking.

Start in Chandler

  1. Check local fit. Ask Kidizona for recent audience geography and confirm which Chandler practices can take new patients.
  2. Show the booking steps. Have Kidizona demonstrate the public search with sample input. Corner Health reviews the script. Disclose the sponsorship in the video and include the city page in the follow-up Story.
  3. Measure completed first visits. Watch visits to the public page for early feedback. Compare pilot cost with completed first visits using Corner Health’s approved aggregate reporting. Patient details stay in Corner Health’s systems.
