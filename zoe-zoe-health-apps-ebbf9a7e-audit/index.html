How this page was built
  • Video search ran on Apify’s YouTube search (grow_media/youtube-search-api) with this page’s window, region and language applied. Every video, channel and paid-label figure came from the YouTube Data API v3 (videos.list, channels.list), read on 19 October 2026.
  • Beyond YouTube, this page read Instagram paid-partnership labels from Meta’s branded-content records for the 1 of 6 brands whose Instagram account we could verify (linked from the brand’s own website, and linking back to it or carrying Meta’s verification); an account we could not verify was skipped, never guessed; and the latest posts of TikTok and Instagram creators found through category searches and competitors’ partnerships, through Apify, read on 19 October 2026. Public pages only: no logged-in session, no creator contacted, no third-party estimate.
  • TikTok’s sponsored toggle names no brand, so it never counts: a post counts for a brand only when its caption discloses the deal and names the brand, or Meta’s paid-partnership label names it.
  • TikTok and Instagram carry no creator country, so their creator rows count only on a market defined by language and never on one defined by country.
  • The sample is twelve category searches, page one of each. A search returns at most 50 videos, so every count here is a floor, never a market size: 76 distinct videos across 63 channels, published in the 180 days to 19 October 2026. The twelve category searches returned 497 videos inside the window, 131 of them long-form. 55 of them are excluded from the sample: 19 did not pass the market-language test, because a search language preference is a bias and not a filter, 15 name none of this page’s category words, on a channel that publishes a different category and 21 name none of this page’s category words, on a channel this sweep did not read. YouTube’s own total-results figure is never printed.
  • A placement is a window video, long-form and five minutes or longer, where a researched brand won a disclosure phrase within 250 characters of its own name. Every competitor count on this page carries that five-minute floor, and the 03 map counts your own deals without it, so a shorter sponsored video of yours is named rather than dropped. The platform paid label on its own attributes to nobody, and a phrase that names its party inline is spent on that party. A firm placement carries the paid label or a sponsor sentence; a creator-code-only disclosure is counted separately as a code deal, and only with the brand’s own code or offer on the brand’s own line: a line the creator calls affiliate or non-affiliate, a video whose creator says no brand paid, a referral or affiliate-programme link, a link to somebody else and a livestream are never a deal. A brand named with no attributable disclosure is a mention, never a placement: 89 mentions were discarded on that rule.
  • Before any of that, each channel’s standing description text is blanked out: any line or 80-character run appearing in at least half of its stored descriptions, once it has five of them. An evergreen affiliate link counts as standing text, so a brand that survives only inside it is recorded and not counted as a deal: 0 window videos landed there. Two exceptions, as the build review read them: on a paid-labelled video a repeated line carrying the brand’s own offer counts when nothing in the video ties the label to anyone else, and on any video a line in which the creator states that the brand sponsors or partners with them counts beside the brand’s own link or code.
  • We read each channel’s 50 most recent uploads, so every per-channel count is a floor as well. A channel’s median is the views of the last 10 long-form uploads it published inside the window, where long-form means at least 300 seconds and not a livestream, and on an even count the median is the mean of the two middle values. Fewer than 5 leaves no median and no tier: 96 read channels sit there, unmeasured rather than under the floor. Shorts under 60 seconds are counted once, separately, and sit in no median: 224 in the window sample. A channel publishing more Shorts than long-form inside the window is held out of the roster; six of them still plot on the chart, marked held out. A roster row needs a median of at least 3,000 and a long-form upload inside 90 days, an owner path we can point at (a channel description, a link or an email in the descriptions we read, or a custom handle), and a reasoned check that its uploads are about this category and speak to the people who choose or pay for it; the category words only mark a channel for that check, and never decide a roster seat alone. The sample is held to the category words, video by video.
  • the UK and US means any channel that clears the language floor alone, because no receipt this page rests on evidences a country: at least 70 per cent of the window uploads that carry an audio-language field are in English, over at least three of them, or, below three, at least 70 per cent of the window titles classify as English on a fixed stopword list. If a channel tags an audio language at all and not one of those tags is English, it fails outright and the title test is never reached. The market rule is the language floor, and it runs twice. The roster and the chart are held to it channel by channel; the sample is held to it video by video, on each video's own audio tag or title. This page applies no country gate. The placement counts are read across every channel the searches surfaced, in any country and any language, because a competitor’s spend is evidence wherever it sits.
  • The category this page reads is nutrition, gut health and food-tracking apps. The twelve searches that ran are four searches, each run once as written and once with the name of each of the two storefront countries added (“gut health diet”, “ultra processed food”, “healthy eating tips nutrition” and “food tracker app review”; the countries are United Kingdom and United States). A video is on-category when its title carries one of this page’s 24 category words, or its channel clears the same floor over its ten window titles.
