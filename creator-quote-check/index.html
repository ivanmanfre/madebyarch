How to use the worksheet

Read the proposal and enter what it actually includes. Leave missing information blank. The worksheet adds stated costs and gives you questions to take back to the supplier. It does not rank creators or estimate a fair market price.

When terms differ, agree a common requirement before judging the totals. Check the audience evidence and measurement plan separately. A lower total alone cannot establish which creator will perform better.

“Download worksheet” saves a CSV file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets. “Save as PDF / print” includes both proposals and your decision. This page does not store or send your entries.
