How this page was built
  • Everything here came off public YouTube pages read on 13 August 2026: search-result pages, channel about tabs, and video watch pages. No API key, no paid tool, no logged-in session, no estimate from a third-party analytics vendor.
  • Coverage volume is YouTube’s own estimated-results figure at its own upload-date filters. It is an estimate and it wobbles; we say so rather than round it into a headline.
  • The 30-day sample is 14 queries against the last-30-days filter, page one of each, de-duplicated to 104 distinct videos across 46 channels. Treat it as a floor. Percentages in section 04 describe the sample and nothing wider.
  • Subscriber counts, lifetime view counts, join dates and countries are read from each channel’s about tab. View counts and dates are read from each video page.
  • Sponsorship is inferred only from YouTube’s own Includes paid promotion disclosure. We do not guess at who paid, and we do not count a creator as sponsored because a video is complimentary.
  • Ratios and percentages are marked as derived and both inputs are sourced.

What this deliberately does not contain: watch time, audience demographics, CPM or rate-card estimates, sentiment scored by a model, or any figure we could not open in a browser and point at. View counts drift upward. The numbers on this page are a snapshot, and every one of them is a few hundred views behind by the time you read it.
